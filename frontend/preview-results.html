Grammar Corrections

Les batiments seraient couverts de jardins Les batiments seraient recouverts de jardins

"Recouverts" is more precise here as it implies the gardens completely cover the surfaces, matching the article's description of facades being covered.

Les transports seraient entierement electriques Les transports en commun seraient entierement electriques

Adding "en commun" specifies public transportation, which is what the article discusses. "Transports" alone is more general.
